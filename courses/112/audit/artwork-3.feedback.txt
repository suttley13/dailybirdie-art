The geometry is sound, but the current artwork is not publishable. Its putting greens—most clearly the island green—read as concentric target-like rings rather than one natural close-mown surface with a narrow, low-contrast fringe. The bridge to the island also suggests a dry route onto a hole whose geometry is an island-green par 3. These are artwork issues, not reasons to alter playable geometry, so no geometry edits are requested. The course itself offers a cape par 4, a dry three-shot par 5 and an island par 3, with credible safe options and a fair challenge. Island greens and related water-hole themes recur in the recent rounds, but the dry middle hole and this sequence provide sufficient variety.
