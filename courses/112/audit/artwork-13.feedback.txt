The numbered routing maps to the artwork: the lower cape par 4, upper dry three-shot par 5, and island-green par 3 are all identifiable, and their major water relationships and playable corridors are credible. Strategically and in difficulty, the geometry is sound: the cape offers a water-side risk/reward choice, the par 5 supports staged play, and the island hole is a distinct carry test. The par 4/5/3 sequence gives strong variety; island greens recur in recent rounds, but the full combination remains sufficiently fresh. However, the CURRENT artwork does not meet the required putting-green treatment. The greens have conspicuous dark, thick outlined rims and broad, high-contrast collars; the island green especially reads as a raised, ringed platform rather than one natural sage/apple-green close-mown surface with a narrow, restrained fringe. Reject for artwork regeneration. No geometry edit can safely correct this rendering mismatch.
